Overview: new layout

The Overview answers three questions, in order: is it working, what does it look like, how does it behave. Everything else is one click away in the other tabs or the node menu.

Proposed order

ALive block. Status, particles alive, emission, lifetime, pool fill, a 10 s history line and the colour ramp. One block instead of a hero card, three stat tiles and a spectrum section. It turns amber and says why when the pool is full.
BOne trigger. The effect's own action sits in the live block and its label adapts: Burst, Throw ball, Next shell, Fire salvo, Replay. The transport button becomes Burst all, so the two no longer share a name.
CLook. The effect's signature choice (aperture, firework shape, grid shape…) comes first, as plain choice tiles.
DBehaviour. Three switches in one list. The wind coupling value appears under its switch only when wind is on. Locked rows say why.
ERecipe. A read-only summary of the setup, with links straight to Source and Motion & FX.
FNode menu. The ··· button next to the switch holds Duplicate, Clear, Reset to preset, Frame and Delete, off the page. The switch and menu sit on the crumb row so the name gets the full width.
