What is new in each

A Lifeline. No cards. One colour line runs from birth to death; each stage opens in place or shows a one-line summary. Rows are the sliders (the hairline fills). The live chart shows the age of every particle.
B Bench. Square modules on a 4-column rack, LED latches instead of switches, step faders, a level meter. Category rail on the left edge. Everything fits without scrolling.
C Spec sheet. Settings written as sentences. Each underlined value opens a ruler to drag. Amber dots mark changes from the preset.
