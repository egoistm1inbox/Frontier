Surface-aware detail

Opaque backfaces can be culled from this view. They must remain available to reflection and shadow rays.

01Local selection

72 parametric patches independently choose a subdivision level. Real triangle counts come from the generated mesh, not a drawn overlay.

02Boundary agreement

Neighboring patches share edge samples and stitch different levels. UV island boundaries remain fixed; this is not a general UV-aware simplifier.

03Transmission policy

Glass-safe mode keeps the entire closed shell at maximum detail. Transparency here is illustrative—not a physically traced refraction test.
