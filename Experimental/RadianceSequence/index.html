Method & limitations

Each cascade traces a finite shell around every sample in a real 3D lattice. Each sample covers all six cubemap faces. RGBA stores radiance and transmittance, not irradiance.

Far-to-near merge: L = Lnear + Tnear × Lfar; T = Tnear × Tfar. Eight spatial samples and four finer angular cells are interpolated with cubemap solid-angle weights.

Diffuse iterations feed the previous solution back at triangle hits. The first iteration is emission-only direct area lighting. Further iterations add reflected transport, not extra ambient light.

Receiver shading compresses the merged directional field to six positive, axis-oriented cosine integrals and samples a live 16 × 16 lighting chart per face. This is an illumination cache, never a geometry proxy. Scene edits clear and recompute it.

Known limits: coarse-lattice light leaks and interpolation bias, receiver angular blur, undersampling of tiny emitters, finite 18.75 m range, and delayed multi-bounce updates. Seven convex solids / 84 actual triangles; not a large-scene benchmark. No claim of parity with production GI.
