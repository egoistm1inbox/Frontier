Method & scope

Landform presets establish cliff proportions. Gradient, ridged and cellular noise vary the crown, bays and cross-sections coherently. Formation and fracture seeds are independent. The same seeds reproduce the same geometry.

Kinked joints enter all four exposures and terminate against an interior core. Stage 2 offers conjugate fractures, block jointing, steep joints or optional bedding. Non-bedded cuts split selected volumes, rather than drawing rows across the entire cliff. Local edge spalls cut four- or six-facet cavities while preserving both edge ends. Surface fissures are closed-bottom polygon V-grooves.

Constrained planar triangulation and bounded topology cleanup. This is a geological modelling tool, not a fracture-mechanics or erosion simulation. SDF erosion is excluded.
