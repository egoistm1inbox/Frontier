# SolidArc Set 3 plan — bounded partial plane–cone root chamfer

## Bounded next slice

Extend partial curved-root chamfers to one measured plane–cone sector:

- accept a canonical open circular root chain shared by a planar annular shoulder and a native coaxial conical frustum;
- support one physical half-turn/general non-reflex sector with explicit radial endpoint caps;
- derive the cone contact from the measured frustum slant and rebuild exact shoulder, conical chamfer band, retained cone, and caps;
- require one genus-zero manifold with exact half-turn/general-sector topology;
- verify angular-fraction volume, chain-seed invariance, source immutability, refusals, console dispatch, and visible proof.

## Explicit non-goals

Reflex sectors, arbitrary trimmed arcs, mixed cone/cylinder partial roots, non-coaxial or oblique supports, branched or
incomplete chains, curved networks/corner patches, concave/non-convex networks, and general intersection/trim/sew healing
remain explicit refusals.
