# SolidArc Set 3 plan — bounded partial apex plane–cone chamfer

## Bounded next slice

Extend the bounded apex route to one canonical coaxial partial sector:

- accept one open non-reflex circular root chain shared by a planar annular shoulder and a native coaxial cone ending at a true apex;
- support the canonical half-turn and one general sector with explicit radial endpoint-cap healing;
- measure the root, outer wall, finite cone height, zero-radius endpoint, and cap frame structurally;
- reconstruct exact partial outer/shoulder/chamfer/apex-cone surfaces and preserve the apex as one vertex;
- require exact genus-zero topology, apex-aware meridian volume, source immutability, console dispatch, refusal boundaries,
  and visible proof.

## Explicit non-goals

Non-coaxial or oblique supports, apex networks, arbitrary trimmed/reflex/branched/incomplete roots, curved corner patches,
freeform supports, and general intersection/trim/sew healing remain explicit refusals. This slice does not broaden the
complete apex route or the partial plane–cone route to arbitrary zero-radius geometry.
