Fixed here — the C++ must follow

  • Visibility is an eye, open vs slashed, always on screen. It was a Shown / Hidden chip pair inside the expand, which gave two widgets for one fact and hid the state until you opened the card. The expand's Visible row is gone.
  • Blend is a dropdown, showing all seven of BLEND_MODES. As a chip strip it could only fit the first four, so Darken and Linear Dodge were unreachable.
  • Bubble + timeline: the layer's tag hue is now an Ln identity chip on a continuous track, instead of a 4px strip buried inside the row. A hidden layer greys its bubble and its track segment, so the stack reads at a glance.
  • Micro-animation both ways. Every fly-out grows out of its face and collapses back into it — the exit runs on the live node and the re-render waits on animationend, because a straight re-render would rip the panel out before the close could be seen.
  • Drag a layer to reorder it. Press a row and move: it ghosts in place, an accent line marks the gap it will drop into, and release commits through RelocateLayer. Focus follows the layer. The drag arms only after 4px so a plain click still just focuses.
  • Sliders and dropdowns are ControlsGallery's, not invented here — see below.

Controls copied from ControlsGallery.exe

Transcribed from ValueSlider.cpp, Dropdown.cpp and ControlLayout.cpp, with the metrics from ThemeConfiguration.cpp:104-117:
  • Row height 30px, label column 0.40, side segment 30px, pill rounding 999 — i.e. a true capsule.
  • A slider is pill on the LEFT at 42%, a 12px gap, then the track. This page had them reversed before. The track is 0.72× the row height; the knob's radius is half the track plus 1px, ringed in 35% black.
  • Both the pill number and the track drag. The pill moves at range/400 per pixel; the track jumps absolutely to the pointer.
  • Pills and tracks are borderless — the C++ says so twice: "the pill reads as a flat filled capsule", "no track outline".
  • A dropdown head is a value pill with a grey > cap. Its items are sharp-cornered, hover paints ControlActive plus a 4px blue bar on the left and slides the label 14px→20px, and every row carries a radio — a grey ring, or a blue ring with a filled centre when current. A tick mark would be wrong.

What the C++ claims about the card

  • An open row + its expand are ONE card. The row is the head: it owns top/left/right and drops its bottom edge; the expand closes the bottom.
  • An open head stops recolouring on hover, so the head can't light alone and split the card.
  • The opacity pill is a drag (ew-resize), not a click.
  • The caret toggles the fold without touching focus; clicking the row body focuses and opens.
