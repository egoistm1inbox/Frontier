Elevation is the angle above the horizon. 0 = sunrise, 1.57 = overhead.

Exposure

🔴 Photographic stops, not a raw multiply. 0 EV is calibrated so a clear noon sky sits mid-frame without clipping. ±1 EV halves/doubles.

Camera

Hold right mouse to look, then WASD to fly · Q/E (or C/Space) down/up · Shift boost · wheel = speed. Altitude is metres, so 12000 puts you well above the deck.

Atmosphere

Clouds

Coverage shrinks clouds rather than fading them — that is the remap, not a multiply. Fly to ~1.5–3.5 km to enter the deck. Sun/ambient gain are exposure headroom, not physics — the sun colour and sky colour reaching a cloud both come from the atmosphere LUTs now, so clouds redden at dusk on their own.
