“Trace only the silhouette, not the whole object”

Good instinct for rendering an outline — but a shadow ray asks “am I blocked?”, and blocking happens through the solid interior.

Trace only the outline hollow shadow

Rays through the interior never meet an edge, so they report unblocked. The object casts a hollow ring, not a shadow — light pours through the middle.

Trace the solid filled shadow

The occluder blocks because it’s filled. A hand blocks a flashlight by being solid, not by having an outline — the palm casts the shadow, and the palm isn’t on the silhouette.

The idea that DOES work: spend rays only at the shadow’s edge

Your instinct — “don’t pay everywhere” — is right; it just applies to the shadow’s edge, not the object’s. The umbra (fully dark) and the lit region both give the same answer from 1 ray as from 8. Only the penumbra (soft edge) needs the extra taps. So: fire 1 cheap any-hit ray per pixel; only where a neighbourhood straddles lit↔shadowed do you fire the remaining N. Exact result, ~8× fewer rays across the flat interior — and it stacks on top of the any-hit early-out.
