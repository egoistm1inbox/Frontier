Viewport header — redesign

Option A is shipped. Built in Engine/Editor/ViewportPanel.cpp::RecordBar — three zones, the mode pill over the existing transport state, pause/step/stop eased in only while something runs, the status readout (Live / Static / Held + accumulated samples, hover for the restart reason) and the 2 px convergence hairline. B and C are kept below as the alternatives that were weighed.

Three options drawn with the engine's own tokens (ViewportPanel.cpp colours, 26 px controls, 13 px radius, 10.5 px tracked caps). Everything below is live — click the modes and the toggles.

Option A — three zones, transport on demand · recommended

Left is the scene, centre is the run, right is the view. The mode switch replaces five buttons and the state chip: it is the state. Pause / step / stop slide in only while something runs. The right zone owns how you look at the scene, and a 2 px hairline under the rail carries the accumulation.

Option B — one primary action

Fewer pixels still: a single filled action button that is the next thing you would do (Play → Pause → Resume), with the mode chosen from its own small caret. Transport lives in the overflow. Good if the rail has to share the row with a future toolbar; less discoverable than A.

What it costs to build (option A)

PieceWhere it lands
Mode pillViewportPanel::RecordTransport — replaces kRuns[5] and the state chip with three segments over the existing Transport_ / Paused_ state. No new state.
Contextual transportSame function; drawn only when Transport_ != kStop, width eased with the panel's existing fade idiom (ControlPanel::FadeTint already animates this way).
ZonesThree cursors instead of one right-anchored run: left at Cursor.x, centre at Cursor.x + (RowWidth - CentreW)/2, right anchored as today.
Status + hairlineNeeds the accumulation index and the restart reason — ReSTIRIntegrator::QueryAccumulationIndex() and QueryRestartReason(), both of which now exist. The hairline is one AddRectFilled.
RealtimeFolds into the status: Live (realtime) / Static (paused clock) / Held. One click toggles, the label says which.
RemovedTwo dock glyphs merge into one segmented pair; the separate “Edit/Play/Paused” chip and the standalone Realtime pill both disappear.
Built. Every row of the table above landed in Engine/Editor/ViewportPanel.cpp; the status and the hairline read QueryAccumulationIndex() / QueryRestartReason() through ViewportPanel::AssignRenderStatus, seated each frame by RenderScheduler::Present. Not yet seen on a GPU here — it compiles warning-clean and the panel's own proof cannot link in this sandbox (it needs thorvg), so the first look is on your machine.
