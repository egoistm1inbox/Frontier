## Accepted construction

Accept one finite straight edge, two explicit positively oriented planar support directions
perpendicular to it, one strict non-reflex angle, unequal positive support widths, one genuinely
nonlinear positive quadratic radius law, and one fixed transition angle leaving the oblique core.

The verifier must establish:

4. closed one-hull genus-zero topology, outward normals, and integrated radius-square volume;
5. transactional refusals for linear/non-positive/consuming laws, orthogonal/invalid frames,

## Unsupported boundary

pure quarter-circle G2-to-plane joins, arbitrary B-rep rolling-ball G2, and healing remain
explicit refusals. Stages 4j and 4k remain unchanged.
