# Stage 4k — bounded quadratic variable-radius G2 rolling-ball core

Extend the completed Stage 4i perpendicular rolling-ball-core G2 route along one explicit complete
straight edge. This is a distinct variable-radius construction and does not relabel the linear
foundation or the constant-radius G2 routes.

## Accepted construction

Accept one finite straight edge, two perpendicular planar support directions, equal positive support
widths, and one genuinely nonlinear positive quadratic radius law. At the start, middle, and end
stations, the section contains the Stage 4i quintic zero-curvature support transitions and an exact
rational circular core. Fixed homogeneous station surfaces preserve the quadratic radius law and
shared section boundaries; the retained support/outer surfaces are fixed and the finite ends are
closed by the existing sewing route.

## Acceptance checks

The verifier must establish:

1. genuinely nonlinear positive radius values and sampled tangent-distance feasibility;
2. exact start/middle/end section radii, rational core presence, and zero/`1/r(t)` G2 joins;
3. deterministic closed genus-zero topology, outward normals, and positive volume;
4. integrated removed-corner area proportional to the radius-square law;
5. transactional refusals for linear/non-positive/consuming laws, oblique frames, partial ranges,
   degenerate dimensions, and apex requests;
6. a distinct variable-versus-constant exterior proof.

## Unsupported boundary

Oblique variable-radius G2, arbitrary edge selection, curved/freeform supports, variable-radius
apexes, pure quarter-circle G2-to-plane joins, arbitrary B-rep rolling-ball G2, and healing remain
explicit refusals. Stages 4i and 4j remain unchanged.
