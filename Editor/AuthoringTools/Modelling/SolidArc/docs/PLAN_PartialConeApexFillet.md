# Stage 4d — bounded partial coaxial conical-apex fillet

Extend the exact Stage 4b cone-apex spherical cap to one explicit non-reflex coaxial sector.
The cone, spherical cap, and base disk are partial revolutions over a strict sweep angle; two
planar meridian caps close the sector. The route is intentionally a partial sector descriptor,
not arbitrary apex selection or mixed-support healing.

## Acceptance boundary

- one right circular cone, one analytic spherical apex cap, and one planar base sector;
- strict partial sweep `0 < SweepAngle < 2π`, with a canonical half-turn proof fixture;
- two planar meridian caps explicitly close the radial boundaries;
- same tangent-circle fit bound, analytic frustum-plus-cap sector volume, closed genus-zero
  topology, normals, and transactional refusals;
- no general vertex selection, partial/mixed-support apex networks, non-coaxial supports,
  rolling-ball G2 continuity, or arbitrary healing.
