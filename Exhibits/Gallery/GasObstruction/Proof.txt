NATIVE GAS OBSTRUCTION - the three collision levels, and who consented to each

① The opt-in is the deliverable: nothing obstructs until an object says so
  PASS  a freshly made record obstructs nothing and is pushed by nothing - every default is off
  PASS  and all three levels agree about it
  PASS  ticking the box without giving it a shape still obstructs nothing - that is an unfinished crate, not an error
  PASS  with a box it obstructs through level ①
  PASS  and not through level ②, which is a different answer
  PASS  an object sent to the distance field is not also walked as a primitive - that would be two admissions of one crate
  PASS  ⑤ and none of that turned the two-way switch on

The routing rule: analytic shapes take level ①, everything else takes level ②
  PASS  a crate is a box, and a box is level ① - 32768 clipmap samples to rediscover six planes is the wrong answer
  PASS  terrain is not a sphere, so it is level ② - and it never moves, so the clipmap never restages
  PASS  giving an object a primitive moves it to level ① in the same breath, with no second switch to forget
  PASS  and taking it away moves it back
  PASS  ① routing an object is not consenting for it: it still obstructs nothing until somebody ticks the box
  PASS  a car that is parked for good may be composited into the world like any other scenery
  PASS  ⚠️ but a car at 100 km/h is read in its own space instead - the composited world is stale the moment it moves
  PASS  and so is one creeping either way: anything that moves perceptibly is better asked than rebaked
  PASS  a wheel that is already a ring is analytic and has nothing to read locally
  PASS  which is what GasColliderShape::TyreRing was for: a thing this simple stays level ①

⑤ The two-way switch is read from exactly one place
  PASS  the object's own record wins over a stale toggle in the tuning - one switch, one place
  PASS  while the authored tuning survives intact
  PASS  so a barrel nobody opted in feels exactly zero newtons in a six metre a second flow
  PASS  and the same barrel, opted in, is pushed along the flow

② The cull is by scene order, and drops its tail rather than reordering
  PASS  three of six are walked: one is far away, one never opted in, one is level ②
  PASS  and they come out in scene order with the gaps closed, not in whatever order they were found
  PASS  a cramped list is filled to its room
  PASS  ② and it is the TAIL that is dropped - the same two survive on every machine with this scene
  PASS  a scene with no records walks nothing

Level ①: a sphere in the plume, and the plume goes around it
  PASS  the boulder is in the lattice
  PASS  and an unobstructed field has nothing solid in it at all
  PASS  🔴 no smoke is inside the boulder - gas inside geometry is a hole in the world the player can see through
  PASS  less smoke reaches the top of the box than without it, because some of it went around
  PASS  but not none: a 55 cm boulder splits a plume, it does not cap a chimney

③ Occupancy is rebuilt every advance, so a moved obstruction leaves no wake
  PASS  a platform at one metre fills some voxels
  PASS  raised, it fills about the same number - nothing like the sum of where it has been
  PASS  ③ and where it used to be is open air again, with no solid wake behind it

④ A moving obstruction pushes the air in front of it, through its boundary
  PASS  a door standing still moves no air at all
  PASS  ④ swung at three and a half metres a second, it writes into the air around it
  PASS  ④ and never inside itself, where the solver would still the air in the same advance
  PASS  every push landed in open air, which is the only place it can carry from
  PASS  and smoke beside a swinging door is driven away from it, where a parked one leaves it

Level ②: the engine's own global distance field, with no primitive anywhere
  PASS  🔴 an L-shaped wall obstructs the gas with no primitive authored for it - the raster already knew the shape
  PASS  and a level with no distance field admits nothing rather than refusing to run
  PASS  the wall is in the gas lattice as well as in the raster
  PASS  and no smoke is inside it
  PASS  the plume reaches the ceiling less readily with a wall over it than without one
  PASS  the two levels compose in one admission, in one field
  PASS  and the sphere adds solid voxels the wall did not already own

🔴 A moving body is asked in its own space, never restaged - and so it may also turn
  PASS  a point along the plank's length is inside it
  PASS  and one along its width is outside it
  PASS  🔴 turned a quarter turn, the plank is now inside where it was outside - rotation the composited world cannot express at all
  PASS  and outside where it was inside
  PASS  and sliding the body slides what the gas sees, with nothing restaged anywhere
  PASS  a half-size plank no longer reaches 0.6 m along itself
  PASS  the plank is in the gas lattice, read straight out of its own space
  PASS  and moving it moves the solid voxels - without one call to UpdateGlobalGrid in this whole section
  PASS  a plank spinning on the spot still drags the air beside it
  PASS  with a velocity that comes from the spin crossed into the arm, not from a translation it has not made
  PASS  while a plank that is doing nothing moves no air at all
  PASS  a point far from everything is outside everything
  PASS  and a reading with no scene behind it answers 'nothing near' rather than crashing a solver

The whole obstruction step is reproducible
  PASS  sixty advances past a moving boulder are bit-identical on a repeat, occupancy included
