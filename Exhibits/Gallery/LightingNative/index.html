Seven emitters drawn by Engine/Editor/LightInspectorPanel.cpp, converted 1:1 from Experimental/ProjectZeroEditor/LightPanel.js, LightPanel.css and LightProjection.js — the light panel that ships inside index.html. Every capture below is written from real ImGui draw commands by python3 Exhibits/Workbench/Lighting/RunNativeEmitterCards.py, not mocked up.

Each emitter is shown at the two widths an inspector column actually gets. At 318 px the axis fields clip their leading digit, which is what the browser does as well: .step .f carries min-width:0, so flexbox shrinks the field past its 38 px basis and the input clips. The 420 px capture shows them whole.

The run asserts the arithmetic the cards print, taken from the reference: a 10 W driver at 110 lm/W yields 1,100 lm; a 2.4 m strip yields 2,400 lm, draws 34.6 W, carries 144 emitters and pulls 1.44 A; a 14 cd point reads 0.56 lx at five metres and 0.14 lx at ten; a 62 cd spot pools 2.31 m across at five metres; a 2 x 1 m softbox reads 1,200 lm/m2; a 1.5 m tube reads 1,200 lm/m. Executed commands are in Commands.json, source and capture digests in Hashes.json, and the run log in Proof.txt. Port register: Docs/InspectorDepotConversion.md. Header glyphs are the engine's own icon library, baked by BakeLightGlyphs.mjs.
